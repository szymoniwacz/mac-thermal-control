# Roadmap

Phases describe intended sequencing, not executable authorization. Bootstrap readiness was rechecked after merged PR #8; R-001 A/Y is owner-approved; scoped implementation readiness and operation-specific safety gates still apply. [Goal #2](https://github.com/szymoniwacz/mac-thermal-control/issues/2) delivered the [Monterey investigation](../../docs/monterey-telemetry-research.md). The owner approved S-001: A (Swift/Foundation with a minimal reviewed C boundary) and the current definition; see [decisions](decisions.md#owner-approvals--2026-11-13). [Goal #7](https://github.com/szymoniwacz/mac-thermal-control/issues/7) delivered bootstrap in merged PR #8. [Goal #9](https://github.com/szymoniwacz/mac-thermal-control/issues/9) rechecks readiness and prepares the [R-001 discovery proposal](../../docs/discovery-specification.md), without adopting it or implementing product behavior in that historical goal. The owner subsequently accepted A/Y (D-019); Goal #11 implements a synthetic report core/CLI before live public-source adapters.

## Deferred until the relevant gate

Phase 7 delivers the required user-friendly macOS GUI; framework and detailed interaction/accessibility design remain later decisions. This requirement cannot be deferred out of final product completion. Broader Intel model/OS coverage, release packaging and signing, persistent helpers, localization, and advanced optimization remain deferred. Model-specific safety evidence cannot be inferred from success on the initial Mac.

Human-reviewed PRs, macOS CI where feasible, and validation on the actual MacBookPro11,5 apply to future implementation. Signed releases remain deferred.

If discovery cannot provide critical telemetry, report the gap and revisit scope or sensing options. Do not silently cross into writes, privilege escalation, or workload testing to fill it.
